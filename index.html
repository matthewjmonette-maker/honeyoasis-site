The problem

Young B2B tech companies need to sell before they can afford a sales leader.

Most early-stage teams are a handful of people. The founder closes the first deals, then growth stalls when there is no repeatable playbook to hand over.

Founder-led sales stalls

Founders close the first deals themselves, but without a repeatable playbook, growth stops when their time runs out.

Senior talent is scarce

Experienced sales and marketing leaders are expensive and hard to hire this early, especially across several markets.

Consulting doesn’t scale

Advisers rebuild the same ICPs, decks and comp plans by hand for every client, so cost rises with every hour.

Go-to-market expertise has always been sold by the hour. HoneyOasis turns it into a system that scales.
